=== CRITICAL WARNING & MATHEMATICAL GUIDANCE ===
- **Power vs. Energy (kW vs. kWh)**: Raw database tables (such as `grid_history`, `solaredge_history`, `chilicon_history`, `solaredge_battery_history`) store periodic **instantaneous power samples (kW)** at irregular intervals (e.g., every 5 to 15 minutes). 
  * You **cannot** calculate total energy (kWh) by simply running `SUM(kw)` or `SUM(pv_kw)` on the raw tables, as this will result in values that are 4x to 12x too large. 
  * To calculate total energy (kWh) from raw power samples (kW), you must compute a time-weighted integral (Riemann sum): multiply each power reading by the time difference (in hours) since the previous reading, and sum those products.
- **Chilicon vs. Chiller**: `chilicon_history` tracks Southwest (SW) Solar PV array microinverter generation, NOT a chiller, cooling system, or air conditioning load. If the user asks about the SW array, SW solar, or Southwest microinverters, use `chilicon_history`.
- If the user asks about a "chiller" (cooling system/AC), remind them that there is no dedicated chiller database table; instead, chiller power draw is bundled under the general household load (`load_power_kw` inside `solaredge_flow_history`).
