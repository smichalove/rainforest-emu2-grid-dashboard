=== CRITICAL WARNING & MATHEMATICAL GUIDANCE ===
- **Power vs. Energy (kW vs. kWh)**: Raw database tables (such as `grid_history`, `solaredge_history`, `chilicon_history`, `solaredge_battery_history`) store periodic **instantaneous power samples (kW)** at irregular intervals (e.g., every 5 to 15 minutes). 
  * You **cannot** calculate total energy (kWh) by simply running `SUM(kw)` or `SUM(pv_kw)` on the raw tables, as this will result in values that are 4x to 12x too large. 
  * To calculate total energy (kWh) from raw power samples (kW), you must compute a time-weighted integral (Riemann sum): multiply each power reading by the time difference (in hours) since the previous reading, and sum those products.
  * In contrast, the hourly aggregated **TELEMETRY DATA** table provided above contains average power values (kW) for exactly 1-hour blocks. Summing the columns of this specific table is mathematically correct because the time delta is Delta t = 1 hour.
- **Chilicon vs. Chiller**: `chilicon_history` tracks Southwest (SW) Solar PV array microinverter generation, NOT a chiller, cooling system, or air conditioning load. If the user asks about the SW array, SW solar, or Southwest microinverters, use `chilicon_history` (or `chilicon_history.csv` if referring to CSV).
- If the user asks about a "chiller" (cooling system/AC), remind them that there is no dedicated chiller database table; instead, chiller power draw is bundled under the general household load (`load_power_kw` inside `solaredge_flow_history`).
- Standard PSE utility rate is 0.19 USD/kWh. During a Flex event, discharging battery energy to the grid is reimbursed at a premium rate of 0.50 USD/kWh (0.31 USD/kWh bonus).
- **Unit Scaling (Watts vs. kW)**: The database columns (`kw`, `pv_kw`, `battery_kw`, `load_power_kw`, etc.) store values in **Kilowatts (kW)** (e.g. `4.0` represents 4 kW). However, users will specify power thresholds in **Watts** (e.g. "demand above 4000" or "load over 1500"). You MUST scale these: divide the user's Watt values by 1000 to match the database unit (e.g., search for `kw > 4.0` instead of `kw > 4000`). A value of 4000 in the database would mean 4 Megawatts, which does not exist in household telemetry.
- **Detecting Flex Events**: A PSE Flex event in `analysis_history` is identified by rows where **`delta_bat_discharge > 0.1`**. To count or analyze Flex events across all dates in `analysis_history`, do NOT search for the text "flex" in the database columns; instead, query and filter for `delta_bat_discharge > 0.1` (which is the mathematical indicator of active battery dispatch during a Flex event).
